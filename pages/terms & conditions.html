Terms & Conditions

Rules and regulations for hosting and attending university events in Pakistan.

1. Organizer Accountability & HEC Guidelines

All event listings posted by campus student societies, departments, or administration must comply with the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan guidelines. Organizers must secure written NOC (No Objection Certificate) from their respective university management before making an event public on this platform.

2. Mandatory Security & Safety Protocols

Per local administration laws of Pakistan, the host university must manage physical security cordons. Any multi-university tech fest, concert, or sports gala must restrict entry via strict verification of original university student CNIC/Smart Cards alongside the UniEvents PK QR ticket stub.

3. Zero-Tolerance Harassment Policy

Any form of political profiling, violent speech, weapon possession, or workplace harassment within campus event perimeters is strictly illegal under the Protection Against Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act. Violators will face immediate profile blacklisting and reporting to the academic disciplinary committee.

4. Ticketing & Refund Resolution Ledger

Paid ticket registrations processed via localized integrations (Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay) are bound by standard banking clearance timelines. If an event gets canceled due to unexpected administrative lockouts, standard automated validation refunds will be issued back to source accounts within 7 working days.
