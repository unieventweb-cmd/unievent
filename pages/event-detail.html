LUMS Tech Fusion 2026

About This Event

LUMS Tech Fusion is one of Pakistan's largest student-run technology festivals, bringing together over 3,000 students from universities across the country for two days of hackathons, robotics competitions, AI workshops, and startup pitch sessions.

This year's edition features a 24-hour hackathon with a Rs. 500,000 prize pool, guest sessions from leading Pakistani tech founders, and an exhibition hall showcasing final-year projects from computer science departments nationwide.
