OUR STORY

Every great campus event deserves to be found.

UniEvents PK started as a simple frustration: brilliant student-run events across Pakistan's universities were happening in silos, known only within their own campus. We built a single place for all of them.

With over 200 chartered universities and thousands of student societies, Pakistan has no shortage of tech fests, sports galas, cultural nights, and academic conferences. But a student at COMSATS Islamabad had almost no way of knowing about a hackathon happening at IBA Karachi the same weekend — and organizers had no easy way to reach students outside their own campus.

UniEvents PK exists to close that gap: one platform where any verified university society can list an event in minutes, and any student can discover what's happening across the country by city, category, or date.
